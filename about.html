ALL ARE EQUAL

★ Everyone living on this Earth is equal.
Nobody is above or below anyone.
Every human being deserves equal rights, love and respect.
We should always remember this in our lives.

★ We should never judge or discriminate against people based on their colour, religion, language, race or financial status.
Everyone has the right to live freely and happily with their own identity.

★ Respecting one another and understanding others' feelings is everyone's responsibility.
Every person should get equal opportunities in life.
Nobody should be treated as less important than others.

★ Let us live together with love and unity, because we are all equal.
Let's build a better world together.
